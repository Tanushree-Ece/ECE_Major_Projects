# Analog Design Projects

This directory contains my analog and mixed-signal design projects completed as part of my ECE coursework and self-learning.  
The projects focus on transistor-level design, simulation, and performance analysis using industry standard EDA tools.

## Tools and  Technologies
- Cadence Virtuoso 
- Spectre Simulator
- CMOS Analog Design
- DC, AC, and Transient Analysis


##  Projects

### Single Stage Operational Amplifier

Design and simulation of a single-stage CMOS operational amplifier.  
Performance evaluation includes gain, bandwidth, power dissipation, and output swing.
Folder: 'Single_Stage_OpAmp'


### Two Stage Operational Amplifier
Design of a two-stage CMOS operational amplifier with improved gain and output swing compared to single-stage designs.  
Includes frequency response and stability analysis.
 Folder: 'Two_Stage_Opam'

###  Low Dropout Regulator (LDO)
Design and simulation of a low dropout voltage regulator for stable voltage regulation.  
Analysis includes line regulation, load regulation, and no load regulation.
 Folder: 'Low_Dropout_Voltage'


##  Notes
- All designs are implemented at the transistor level.
- Simulation results and reports are included inside individual project folders.
- Technology details are mentioned in each project’s README.
